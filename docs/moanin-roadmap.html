Bar 15 → 16 counts as one gesture: 3, +4, (1, 2) 3, 4 → swing. 8 responses in A1+A2, 4 in A3. Bar 32: keep it simple, no fill. Goal: get this first AABA right — in the solos just play the standard groove.
