Bar 16 → 17 counts as one gesture: 3, +4, (1, 2) 3, 4 → swing. 8 responses in A1+A2, then the fill, 7 bars of swing, catch, one bar of rest, 4 responses. Goal: get this first AABA right — in the solos just play the standard groove.
